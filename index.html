CYBERDECK.TOOLS is a deck of free browser tools for cyberpunk tabletop RPGs — Cyberpunk RED, Cyberpunk 2020, Shadowrun — and for any game master who wants in-world props without opening an image editor.

Everything runs locally in your browser: no signup, no upload, no install. Each program exports a clean PNG, ready for Roll20, Foundry VTT, Discord or print.
